Staff-assisted workflow

Turn a paper form into a digital record.

Photograph a completed sample form, extract text and check each field against the original before exporting.

Prototype only: use fictional information. Recognition may miss handwriting, checkboxes and entire answers. It cannot establish donor eligibility or replace clinical screening. Do not use with real donor records until the blood bank approves the workflow and infrastructure.

1. Add a form

For best results, use a sharp, well-lit image taken straight above the page. JPG, PNG or WebP; one page at a time.

The image stays in this browser. The OCR engine and its English language model are downloaded from third-party servers when you scan; an internet connection is needed.

The original image is not included in the export. Scanning works best on printed text; handwriting and small marks may be unreliable.

2. Review and correct

Compare every value with the paper form. Blank or unclear values must remain blank until confirmed; no checkbox answers are inferred.

This is a small illustrative set of fields, not a complete transcription of the donor form or the blood bank’s actual Excel columns. Record the clinical questionnaire separately under the approved process.

3. Export reviewed records

Records exist only until this page is refreshed or closed. Excel opens the downloaded CSV; its columns are illustrative.
